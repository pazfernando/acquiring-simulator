## commit message (verbatim)
WI-ACQ-OBS-001: access realization rev3, N-02 gate, real JWT auth, console, Terraform and CI

Adopt contracts revision 3 with the D6 clarifications and the DR3-01
correction, superseding the pre-domain revision. Revision 3 keeps the
operation-a delta, the wire/websocket/response vectors and the
input/output identity manifests, with the supersession and verification
records for the D6 and DR3-01 passes.

Close N-02 at authoring with the origin-verification gate: CloudFront
injects a random_password-derived x-origin-verify header on /api/* and
the console compares it byte-exactly, fail-closed, with a denial sink
for rejected origins. The value has no default, no tfvars or CI
variable, no output and no log path; the gate and its negative tests are
recorded under n02-origin-verification evidence.

Add the read-only console history with cursor binding and the failure
taxonomy, so a paged read is bound to the issuing query and rejected or
re-mapped deterministically when the cursor does not match.

Add real JWS/JWKS authentication: compact JWS parsing, JWKS fetch and
parse with a trusted-issuer configuration, and a JWT console token
decoder replacing the demo-only authorizer path. The dev stub token
provider remains explicitly non-authenticating and is confined to the
local development harness.

Author the Terraform stack (bootstrap, stack env, and api, console,
data, identity, keycloak, mtls, network, observability and ws modules)
together with the policy and static checks, and the CI workflows for
build/test and Terraform. The infrastructure and workflows are locally
validated only: fmt, validate, tflint, actionlint and the policy checks
were run locally; nothing is applied.

Include the independent reviews recorded for this slice (domain and
security, for the contract delta, D6 clarifications, continuation rev2,
rev3 and DR3-01, N-02 implementation and origin verification, QA and
recheck passes) with their integrity manifests and evidence directories.

Scope note: this commit publishes authored work only. Nothing is
deployed, applied or released. No PR, merge, tag or release is created
by this commit.
